# Simple User Queries for yf_tools

Simple, natural queries that will invoke each Yahoo Finance tool:

## Price & Basic Data

1. **get_ticker_price**
   - "What's the current price of AAPL?"
   - "Show me the price of Tesla"
   - "How much is Microsoft stock?"

## Ownership & Holders

2. **get_major_holders**
   - "Who are the major shareholders of Apple?"
   - "Show me major holders of TSLA"

3. **get_institutional_holders**
   - "What institutions hold Microsoft stock?"
   - "Show institutional holders of AAPL"

4. **get_mutualfund_holders**
   - "Which mutual funds hold Apple?"
   - "Show mutual fund holders of MSFT"

## Insider Activity

5. **get_insider_purchases**
   - "Show insider purchases for Apple"
   - "Who bought Tesla stock recently?"

6. **get_insider_transactions**
   - "What insider transactions happened in Microsoft?"
   - "Show insider activity for AAPL"

## Returns & Distributions

7. **get_dividends**
   - "What dividends did Apple pay?"
   - "Show dividend history for MSFT"

8. **get_capital_gains**
   - "Show capital gains for SPY"
   - "What capital gains distributions for VTI?"

## Financial Statements

9. **get_balance_sheet**
   - "Show me Apple's balance sheet"
   - "What's the balance sheet of Tesla?"

10. **get_cash_flow**
    - "Show cash flow for Microsoft"
    - "What's Apple's cash flow statement?"

11. **get_income_statement**
    - "Show income statement for AAPL"
    - "What's Tesla's income statement?"

## Earnings & Estimates

12. **get_earnings**
    - "Show earnings for Apple"
    - "What are Microsoft's earnings?"

13. **get_earnings_estimate**
    - "What are the earnings estimates for Tesla?"
    - "Show earnings estimates for AAPL"

14. **get_earnings_history**
    - "Show earnings history for Apple"
    - "What's the earnings history of MSFT?"

15. **get_revenue_estimate**
    - "What are revenue estimates for Tesla?"
    - "Show revenue estimates for Apple"

## EPS Data

16. **get_eps_trend**
    - "Show EPS trend for Apple"
    - "What's the EPS trend for Microsoft?"

17. **get_eps_revisions**
    - "Show EPS revisions for AAPL"
    - "What are EPS revisions for Tesla?"

## Growth & Analysis

18. **get_growth_estimates**
    - "Show growth estimates for Apple"
    - "What are growth estimates for Microsoft?"
